## Documentation and design consequence

The [Live 12 Clip View manual, section 8.2.1](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/clip-view/#clip-and-loop-region-settings)
describes unlooped start-to-end playback and time-based unwarped positions.
Audio looping requires Warp to be enabled. Therefore an initial frame-based
unwarped Session source must be a one-shot. Frame looping must not be presented
as Live's warped audio workflow. Beat-based warped playback remains required
later, with an explicit timing mode and validated warp mapping.

This is documentation evidence, not measured installed-reference semantics.
No sample-level timing, gain, retrigger or route-restoration parity is established.
